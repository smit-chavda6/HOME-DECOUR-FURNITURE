Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to fulfill orders, provide support, and improve your experience:

  • Account data (name, email, phone number, password hash)
  • Order and payment details (billing, shipping, items purchased). Payments are processed by trusted gateways; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data (pages visited, device/browser info, approximate location) via cookies/analytics.
  • Messages you send via our contact form or live chat.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and deliver orders; provide invoices and order updates.
  • Respond to support requests and service inquiries.
  • Improve site performance, security, and features.
  • Send account, security, and service-related communications. Marketing emails only with your consent and you can opt out anytime.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for login/session and preferences (like theme). Optional analytics cookies help us understand site usage. You can control cookies via your browser settings.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We share information only as needed to provide our services or when required by law:

  • Payment processors, shipping partners, and service providers (under strict confidentiality).
  • Legal requests, to protect our rights, users, or the public as allowed by law.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.
  • Request a copy of your data or restrict certain processing where applicable.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We’ll post the updated date below.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@homedecorfurniture.com.

Effective date: September 13, 2025
